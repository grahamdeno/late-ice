Satoshi
Copyright Indian Type Foundry.

These three files are the Satoshi faces served by Fontshare's public CSS for weights 500, 700 and 900:

https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=satoshi@500,700,900&display=swap
https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi

Satoshi-Medium.woff2 is font-weight 500.
Satoshi-Bold.woff2 is font-weight 700.
Satoshi-Black.woff2 is font-weight 900.

The family name inside that stylesheet is Satoshi. A Zodiak face that the same stylesheet also referenced was not copied.

Fontshare publishes Satoshi for use in personal and commercial projects, including websites and apps. These files are not sold here and are not offered as a font product of their own. See https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi for the foundry's terms.

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full text is BigShouldersDisplay-OFL.txt in this folder.

BigShouldersDisplay-latin.woff2 is the Latin subset Google Fonts serves for Big Shoulders Display, weights 700 through 900 (one variable file). This Next.js version's next/font/google module does not export that family (it exports Big Shoulders, a different set of files), so the Display face is loaded with next/font/local instead of being renamed.
